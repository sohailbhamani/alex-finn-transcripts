# Unknown

The 2024 Nissan Frontier is the mid-sized pickup truck that is rugged, capable, and full of technology. For this year, the Frontier features a range of performance and styling updates. The all-new Hardbody Edition model joins the lineup, offering a retro-inspired look that pays homage to the legendary truck from the 80s. It features unique 17-inch heritage-styled alloy wheels and black exterior accents. Under the hood, every Frontier is powered by a 3.8-liter V6 engine producing 310 horsepower and 281 pound-feet of torque. Nissan has also added more available tech, including a 12.3-inch touchscreen display on the PRO grades.
